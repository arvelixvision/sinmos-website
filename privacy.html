Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 | Operator: SHENZHEN SINMOS TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how SHENZHEN SINMOS TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD ("SINMOS", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained through our official B2B export website arvelixvision.com.

We are committed to respecting your privacy in full compliance with international data protection regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable global commercial privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

As a B2B manufacturer and exporter, we only collect business contact information voluntarily submitted by prospective distributors, retailers, wholesalers, and commercial buyers through our quote and inquiry forms:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and company name.
  • Commercial Inquiries: Product models of interest, estimated purchase quantities (MOQ), custom OEM/ODM packaging requirements, and target shipping destinations.
  • Technical & Log Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data such as browser type, operating system, and anonymized user behavior via Microsoft Clarity for user experience optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your inquiry information is used exclusively for legitimate B2B business purposes:

  • Preparing formal factory quotations, sample schedules, and commercial invoices.
  • Communicating technical specifications, product catalogs, and CE/FCC compliance certificates.
  • Fulfilling sample requests and coordinating international logistics and customs paperwork.

4. Strict Non-Disclosure & Zero Resale Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your business information to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

Information is only shared with trusted direct logistics providers (e.g., DHL, FedEx, air/sea freight carriers) strictly necessary to calculate shipping freight or deliver your sample and bulk orders.

5. GDPR Data Rights for International Buyers

If you are based in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of any commercial contact records we hold. You may request record deletion at any time by contacting our export team at sales@sinmos.com.
