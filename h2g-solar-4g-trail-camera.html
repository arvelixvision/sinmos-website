Frequently Asked Questions FAQ · BUYER QUESTIONS

How does the H2G solar and 7800mAh battery power system achieve 365-day autonomy?

The H2G features a heavy-duty dual-power architecture. An industrial 7800mAh lithium rechargeable battery pack is sealed inside the casing, continuously replenished by an integrated high-efficiency solar panel. Just 2 to 3 hours of ambient sunlight daily maintains full charge, while the 7800mAh reservoir delivers over 30 days of continuous operation through severe overcast or rainy weather without user intervention.

What cellular 4G LTE network bands does the H2G support?

The standard H2G model is factory-configured for European and global markets supporting LTE-FDD bands B1/B3/B7/B8/B20. For North American brand buyers and OEM projects, SINMOS provides modular hardware versions compatible with US carrier bands (AT&T and Verizon networks).

How does the H2G compare to traditional trail cameras powered by AA batteries?

Traditional 4G trail cameras require 8 to 12 AA batteries that deplete within 2 to 6 weeks under frequent cellular photo transmission, resulting in recurring battery costs and repeated site visits. The H2G eliminates disposable battery expenses entirely and ensures uninterrupted surveillance in remote hunting lands, timber plantations, and off-grid ranch borders.
