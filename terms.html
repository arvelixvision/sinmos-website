All SINMOS trail cameras undergo strict 100% factory inspection, including image sensor calibration, PIR detection trigger testing, waterproof sealing tests, and a 24-hour burn-in aging cycle prior to dispatch.

  • We provide a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty against functional defects under normal outdoor usage conditions.
