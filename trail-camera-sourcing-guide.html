Wholesale & Private-Label Trail Camera Sourcing Guide

By Arvelix Vision · Published 9 November 2026

How do I source trail cameras for wholesale or private label?

Start with the destination market and the way your customers will retrieve recordings. Compare local-recording, nearby WiFi and cellular models, then test the exact version before approving branding and a bulk order. What are the MOQ and lead time?

Sample orders start from 1 pc. Logo printing and stickers have a minimum order of 100 pcs; custom packaging has a minimum order of 1,000 pcs. Firmware and new hardware projects require a feasibility review and a project-specific MOQ.

In-stock samples are generally ready in 2 days. Private-label production generally takes 15–20 days after sample approval. These are preparation or production estimates, not delivery guarantees; confirm stock, artwork approval, shipping and project timing in your quotation.

What evidence should I request before a bulk order?

  • Original daytime and night-time files, with capture settings and firmware version.
  • The exact model and hardware version, a specification sheet and the app details.
  • Model-specific compliance documents for the destination market; a certificate for another model is not a substitute.
  • Agreed inspection criteria, warranty handling, packaging and shipment details.
  • For battery products, applicable transport documentation confirmed with the freight provider.

See our production and airtightness testing and available compliance documents. An airtightness demonstration is not a substitute for a model-specific IP test report.

How do I turn a shortlist into a quotation?

Send the destination country, intended use, preferred models, sample and bulk quantities, and the required logo or packaging work. For cellular cameras, include the intended carrier and SIM requirements. Keep a written sample approval record so production can be checked against the agreed specification.

Buyer questions

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sample orders start from 1 pc. Confirm model availability, sample price, freight and preparation time with the team.

Does every customization start at 100 pcs?

No. Logo printing and stickers start from 100 pcs; custom packaging starts from 1,000 pcs. Firmware and hardware projects require a separate feasibility review, quotation and MOQ.
