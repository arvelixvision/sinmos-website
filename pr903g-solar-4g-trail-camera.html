Deployment Scenarios & Operational Notes APPLICATIONS

Remote Cabin & Farm Security

Operates entirely off-grid without AC power or home Wi-Fi. Transmits instant photo alerts to mobile devices upon perimeter motion.

Deer & Wildlife Scouting

0.3s trigger captures fast-trotting bucks across game trails. Solar recharging eliminates human scent contamination from frequent battery checks.

Construction & Asset Protection

Straps to poles, fences, or trees in minutes. Weather-sealed IP65 housing withstands sub-zero snow, mud, and torrential rain.

Operational & Connectivity Notes: Ships ready with a pre-installed 4G SIM card. To receive remote photos and video transmissions, users activate flexible monthly or annual cloud storage plans directly through the mobile app. The integrated solar panel requires ambient daytime daylight; in heavy shade or deep winter, the 8×AA backup batteries should be installed. Cellular service requires at least 1–2 bars of LTE tower reception at the camera site.
