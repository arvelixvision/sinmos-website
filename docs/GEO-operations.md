Record the exact product/mode, whether web search was enabled, region and date. Start a fresh conversation, avoid brand-leading prompts, and repeat each question three times where practical.

Keep brand mentions and website citations separate. Save an answer record and the exact cited URLs. Treat results as a sample with variation, not a platform-wide market share estimate. Do not combine platform rates or silently change the question set.

Track referral sessions and qualified inquiries separately; AI referral attribution is incomplete. Ask prospects how they discovered the company. Review competitor citations to identify missing useful evidence, not to copy content. Repeat monthly and assess sustained changes alongside inquiry quality.
